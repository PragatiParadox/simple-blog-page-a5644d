The first thing the instructor told us was that the clay would win. She was right. My first bowl folded in on itself like a tired accordion. So did the second, and the ninth.

## Centering is everything

Before you can shape anything, the clay has to be perfectly centered on the wheel. Too much force and it wobbles; too little and it drifts. The trick, it turns out, is to brace your elbows against your knees and let your whole body become steady.

It is a strange thing to learn with your hands. You can't think your way into it. You just keep trying until, one evening, the lump of clay stops fighting and starts spinning true.

## What I kept

Out of roughly forty attempts, I kept three pieces. One is lopsided. One has a crack I filled with gold paint. One is almost a real bowl. I use all three every day.
